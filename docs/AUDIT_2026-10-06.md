## Confirmed problems that were real and are now closed

### AppLab crash attribution

The recurring post-user-flow failures were caused by broad AndroidRuntime/SystemUI crash attribution in AppLab rather than TrailPath runtime crashes.

Closed in the pinned harness by:
- target-scoped AndroidRuntime fatal classification;
- AndroidRuntime emitter PID association;
- matching target Process/PID requirement;
- target-scoped ANR handling;
- shared classifier across System, Network, Storage, Persistence, Background, Upgrade, Configuration, Resource Pressure and crawler paths;
- persisted attribution evidence and self-tests for foreign/interleaved/real target fatal records.

CI #835 reconfirms the complete TrailPath matrix passes with the corrected classifier.

### Small-screen/accessibility determinism

Previously failing constrained UI checks included real and harness-side issues:
- onboarding CTA could become unreachable in compact/large-text conditions;
- the small-screen emulator geometry accidentally produced an approximately 180×320 dp viewport instead of 360×640 dp;
- Flutter combined-semantics nodes made some Profile/Settings/Units selectors brittle.

Closed by the pinned candidate/harness. Current constrained UX gate passes at the intended 360×640 dp viewport with 130% text scaling.
